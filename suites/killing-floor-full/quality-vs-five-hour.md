## Read the chart

Higher means better quality; farther right means less allowance consumed. Equal-width horizontal bands represent 350→100%, 100→50%, and 50→0%, so distances across bands are not equal percentage differences. The field-median quality guide is not a passing grade. Observed ranges are not confidence intervals. Fable's hollow marker identifies an estimate.

The quota is for a complete-book chapter-analysis job. Above 100% means more than one fresh allowance consumed across the run's segments. It does not mean five hours of computation. The quality means pool scored repeats, while quota uses the eligible captures selected for the chart.

## What matters here

Sol medium scored 88.98 at 26.00% of its allowance. Opus 5 scored 87.63 at 118.00%. The practical difference is how much other work the allowance leaves room for, not merely which score is higher.

Astra leads quality but draws much more quota. Luna high offers a much lighter recap option; consult its craft score before choosing it for close study of the writing.
